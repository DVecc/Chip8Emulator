- GLM is vendored under `glm/`. Its original license is retained in `glm/copying.txt` and copied to `licenses/GLM.txt` in the Windows package.
- miniaudio by David Reid is vendored as `src/utils/miniaudio.h`. Its original notices remain in that header. The license text is reproduced in `docs/MINIAUDIO_LICENSE.txt` and included in the Windows package.

No ROMs are included in the Windows package. Use your own trusted ROMs.
